Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with care, transparency, and security. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you browse our website, create an account, place an order, or contact our customer support team.

Information We Collect

To provide a smooth shopping experience, we may collect personal and technical information during your interaction with our store.

  • Name, email address, phone number, and shipping or billing address.
  • Order details, payment-related information, and transaction history.
  • Account preferences, wishlist activity, and customer support messages.
  • Device, browser, IP address, and website usage data for analytics and security purposes.

We only collect information that is relevant to operating our ecommerce services, improving customer experience, and meeting our legal and business obligations.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to operate our business efficiently and deliver reliable customer service before, during, and after your purchase.

  • Process orders, confirm payments, and arrange shipping or delivery.
  • Provide customer service, respond to inquiries, and resolve order issues.
  • Send important updates such as order confirmations, shipping notices, and service messages.
  • Improve website performance, product selection, and user experience.
  • Prevent fraud, monitor suspicious activity, and maintain platform security.

Where permitted, we may also send promotional emails about new arrivals, special campaigns, or exclusive offers. You can unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our store and fulfill services on our behalf.

  • Payment gateways for secure payment processing.
  • Shipping and logistics partners for order fulfillment and delivery updates.
  • Technology and analytics providers that support website performance and business insights.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when disclosure is required by law.

All third-party partners are expected to handle data responsibly and only for approved operational purposes.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep your cart active, understand browsing behavior, and improve functionality. These tools help us create a more personalized and efficient shopping experience.

  • Remember login sessions and saved preferences.
  • Maintain cart and checkout functionality.
  • Measure traffic, engagement, and site performance.
  • Support relevant promotions and remarketing activities.

You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the website may not function properly if cookies are restricted.

Data Security and Customer Rights

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. While no online system can guarantee absolute security, we continuously work to maintain a safe shopping environment.

  • You may request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • You may request deletion of your data where legally permitted.
  • You may contact us regarding privacy concerns or account-related requests.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is used, please contact our customer support team. Continued use of our website indicates acceptance of this policy and any future updates posted on this page.
